# InstaAmbulance 🚑

## AI-Assisted Ambulance Discovery and Coordination Platform

### About the Project
InstaAmbulance is a proposed platform that aims to make it easier for people to discover ambulance service providers based on their location and requirements.

### Problem Statement
During medical emergencies, people may face difficulties finding clear and accessible information about ambulance services.

### Proposed Solution
The project explores a location-based platform to help users discover verified ambulance providers and access their contact information.

### AI Integration
AI may be explored to organise provider information and support relevant search results.

### SDG Alignment
SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-being.

### Current Status
Idea development and preliminary research stage.

### Future Scope
- User-friendly interface
- Location-based ambulance discovery
- Verified provider information
- Future integration of reliable availability and tracking data

### Disclaimer
InstaAmbulance is a project concept and is not an emergency service. In an emergency in Telangana, contact 108.
